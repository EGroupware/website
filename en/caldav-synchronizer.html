Advantages of the Outlook CalDAV Synchroniser

With the CalDAV Synchroniser, you can synchronise data from the address book and calendar between EGroupware and common Outlook versions. Supported Outlook versions are 2019, 2016, 2013, 2010 and 2007.

Synchronisation of appointments and contacts

The CalDAV synchroniser makes it easier for EGropware users to integrate Outlook and synchronise appointments, contacts and tasks. This also gives you access to your appointment calendar and contacts stored in EGroupware in your Outlook.

Collaboration with CalDAV Synchronizer

The Outlook CalDav Synchronizer is Free and Open-Source Software (FOSS). The source code is available on Github. In 2019, EGroupware GmbH started the cooperation with Generalize-IT Solutions (Mr. Alexander Nimmervoll) in the context of CalConnect and offers the synchronisation for all users. From CalDAV Synchronizer version 3.5.0 onwards, EGrouware can be added directly to the profile. In addition, as of this version, bugs were fixed that had led to problems when synchronising with EGroupware. We therefore recommend EGroupware users at least version 3.5.0.
