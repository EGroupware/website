Cloud or own server?

EGroupware is available as a cloud service from Germany and for installation on your own server – with the same software. This page helps you decide. And if your requirements change, you simply switch: in either direction.

Decision guide

What suits your team?

Most organisations clearly recognise themselves in one of the two columns.

The cloud suits you if …

  • You have no IT department of your own or want to relieve it.
  • You want to get started quickly – without a server or installation.
  • Updates and backups should be taken care of reliably.
  • You prefer fixed monthly costs to your own hardware.

Your own server suits you if …

  • You want full control of the server – from the operating system to the timing of updates.
  • You want to implement your own customisations or modules directly in the source code.
  • The data must not leave your organisation for legal or internal reasons.
  • You have IT know-⁠how in-⁠house or work with a service provider.
The middle way

Your own server, looked after by the EGroupware team

The data has to stay in-⁠house, but you lack the time for administration? Then the EGroupware team installs and maintains the software on your server via remote maintenance – billed through support contingents.

Many customers combine their own server with a support contingent: they handle day-⁠to-⁠day tasks themselves and have the team that develops the software at their side for more complex questions.

Frequently asked questions about the operating model

Can I switch between cloud and own server later?

Yes, in either direction. It is the same software; we help you move email, calendars, contacts and files. Nordenham city administration, for example, ran EGroupware itself for twelve years and moved to the cloud in 2018.

Can I connect the cloud to my Active Directory and my file server?

Yes. Active Directory or LDAP stay on your premises and are connected via a VPN link between your network and our data centre – you keep managing users and passwords in one place, as before. If you wish, we also connect a local file server via the SMB protocol, so that your on-⁠site file storage is available directly in EGroupware.

Which is cheaper – cloud or own server?

The cloud costs from €24.90 per month or is billed as a flat rate, and includes operation, updates and backups. The Community Edition comes without licence fees, the Enterprise Line starts at €62.50 per month or as a flat rate (EPL Universal) – with your own server, hardware or hosting and the working time for maintenance and backups come on top. Without in-⁠house IT, the cloud is therefore often the less expensive option.

Do I need an IT department for my own server?

You need Linux skills for installation, updates and backups – in-⁠house or via a service provider. Alternatively, the EGroupware team takes care of maintenance via remote access, based on support contingents.

How secure is my data in the cloud?

The data is stored in two data centres in Frankfurt am Main. Access is always encrypted (HTTPS), and two-⁠factor sign-⁠in is available. We back up the data daily and keep 7 daily and 12 weekly backups.
