Community Edition or Enterprise Line?

Both editions share the same core: all applications, open interfaces and the AI features. The Enterprise Line adds features for companies and public bodies and comes with preferred support from the vendor. Which edition fits depends on what you need – and you can switch at any time.

One set of data, two editions

The Community Edition and the Enterprise Line use the same database. Switching therefore needs no migration: emails, appointments, contacts and files stay where they are. You can start with the Community Edition and move to the Enterprise Line later – or go back to the Community Edition if you no longer need the additional features.

The licence for the EPL version purchased or received during the contract term is valid without time limit. Updates of the EPL features are provided during the contract term.

Orientation

Which edition suits you?

Three ways to EGroupware – with the same core features.

Community Edition

Open source without licence fees, on your own server. A good fit if you have IT staff who install, update and secure the system, and the core features are enough.

Enterprise Line

On your own server, with all EPL features and preferred support from the vendor. A good fit if you want to stay on top of data protection, audits and workflows.

EGroupware Cloud

We run EGroupware for you in Frankfurt am Main. All EPL features are included – you only enable what your team needs.

Frequently asked questions about Community Edition and Enterprise Line

Is the Community Edition a cut-⁠down version?

No. The Community Edition contains all applications, the open interfaces and the AI features. The Enterprise Line mainly adds features for data protection, audits and workflows in larger teams, plus preferred support.

How do I switch between the editions?

Both editions use the same database. Switching is therefore possible in both directions without data migration, and your data is preserved. We are happy to help if you wish.

What happens when the EPL contract ends?

You keep using the EPL version purchased or received during the contract term without time limit. New versions and updates of the EPL features are only provided during the contract term.

Can I get support for the Community Edition?

Yes, from a support package at €350 per hour. For Enterprise Line and cloud customers, the hour costs €175. If you need help regularly, the Enterprise Line is therefore often the cheaper option.
