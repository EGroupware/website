What is EGroupware?

EGroupware is a collaboration solution from Germany that brings calendar, email, file storage, address book, CRM, project management, chat and video conferencing together in a single web interface. You run it either as a cloud service from two data centres in Frankfurt or on your own server. We have been developing EGroupware for more than 20 years.

What is EGroupware?

One platform instead of many isolated tools

EGroupware brings every­thing a team needs day to day into a single web interface. All information is connected: the appointment knows the customer, the task knows the project, the email is filed with the right record. That saves time, avoids duplicate data entry and makes knowledge available across the team.

  • Runs in any modern browser on any operating system – nothing to install on the desktop.
  • Modular by design: activate only the applications you actually need.
  • Open interfaces to Active Directory, LDAP, Univention, CalDAV and CardDAV.
  • Scales from a five-person office to an administration with thousands of accounts.

Frequently asked questions about EGroupware

What is EGroupware?

EGroupware is open source groupware for teams. Calendar, email, file storage, address book, CRM, projects, time tracking, chat and video conferencing run in one shared web interface – as a cloud service from Germany or on your own server.

Is EGroupware an alternative to Microsoft 365?

Yes. EGroupware covers typical office tasks: appointments, email, files, collaborative document editing via Collabora Online, chat and video conferencing. The differences: open source code, hosting in Germany and billing by concurrent users instead of per user. If you depend on specific features of Word, Excel or Teams, compare the feature set before­hand.

How much does EGroupware cost?

The Community Edition is open source and therefore free of licence fees and can be used without limits. Paid options are cloud hosting from €24.90 per month and the Enterprise Line with manufacturer support from €62.50 per month. You will find all details under Pricing.

Where is the data stored?

In the cloud, the data is stored in two data centres in Frankfurt am Main. With your own installation, it is wherever you run the server – in your own data centre or with a hosting provider of your choice.

Can I run EGroupware on my own server?

Yes. EGroupware is a PHP application and runs on your own Linux server, in a private cloud or as an app in the Univention App Center. Either as the licence-free Community Edition or as the Enterprise Line with quality-assured updates and support.

Does EGroupware create e-⁠invoices that meet legal requirements?

Yes. XRechnung and ZUGFeRD according to the EU standard EN 16931 have been included as standard since 2025. Invoices are created directly from project and time data, without additional software.

Who is EGroupware suitable for?

For small teams up to several thousand users: small and medium-sized businesses, trades, schools and universities, associations and non-⁠profit organisations, and public administrations that value control over their data.
