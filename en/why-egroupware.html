Why EGroupware?

Why EGroupware

Save time and money

Increase the effectiveness of your work and that of your entire team with EGroupware. The software networks all information on an online company platform so that all appointments, customer information and tasks can be accessed immediately. EGroupware runs independently of the operating system on every desktop browser and in the mobile version also on smart­phones and tablets. EGroupware offers an online calendar for appointment management, contact management with CRM, task management as well as an online file server, which is rounded off by project management and a ticket system. Home office has never been so easy to realise. Solutions View Solutions

Solutions

Customisable & flexible

The modular structure allows you to activate only the applications you need for your company. This makes it much easier for your users to start with EGroupware. For example, you can start with just the calendar and add more applications over time. With the concept of user-defined fields, categories and types, EGroupware is suitable for every business and can be individually adapted to your customer needs. Do the customisation yourself directly with the graphical user interface without programming knowledge. Solutions View Solutions

Solutions

Cost-effective

EGroupware is open source and uses highly efficient components such as MySQL, MariaDB databases and Apache web servers to operate the software. These components are also open source and therefore save you a lot of licensing costs. This also allows you to trust a secure and scalable basis for your company. The EGroupware Community Edition even comes without licence fees. Through the software subscription EPL you receive extended business functionalities and quality-assured software updates and support. This means that the software can be adapted even better to the requirements of our customers. All customers always have access to the source code of the software and can adapt it if desired. For whom is EGroupware suitable

The software EGroupware in the on-⁠premise installation is suitable for both companies of different sizes, research institutions, educational institutions, universities who have the technical and administrative skills such as Linux, LDAP, Active Directory. The EGroupware Cloud is suitable for all people who want to directly use the benefits of EGroupware on a high data protection standard even without technical knowledge. The EGroupware Cloud is operated from a German data centre. Contact us Contact

Scaling for high demands

Due to the simple integration of the software with existing systems such as Univention, ActiveDirectory or LDAP, EGroupware is quickly available for a large number of users without having to create users multiple times. EGroupware is characterised by easy administration and high availability. Large installations with thousands of users are possible without any problems. Just contact our technicians.
