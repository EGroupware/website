Features

Short paths in the team

Faster than an email, easier to follow than a shout across the office.

Team chats and direct messages

Set up different team chats in Rocket.Chat or write to colleagues directly. That way every discussion stays where it belongs.

Files, audio and video

Exchange files in the chat and switch to a real-time audio or video call when needed. The conversation is transmitted encrypted.

Notification by email or push

If you are not logged in, an email tells you about new messages. With the Rocket.Chat app on your PC or smart­phone you receive push notifications straight on your device.

Enable for users and groups

In the administration you enable the chat for individual users or whole groups. Installation and setup are done directly from EGroupware.

Hosting and apps

In the browser, in the app, on servers of your choice

Rocket.Chat is open source. You install it on your own server or use the EGroupware Rocket.Chat server in a German data centre – so you keep control of your data and chat in compliance with the GDPR. On the move, you chat and make calls with the Rocket.Chat apps for desktop and smart­phone.

Frequently asked questions about the team chat

Do I need a separate account for the chat?

No. You log in via single sign-⁠on with your EGroupware account. The administration decides which users and groups may use the chat.

Are there apps for smart­phone and desktop?

Yes. You download the Rocket.Chat apps for the different platforms from the Rocket.Chat website. Push notifications then inform you directly on your device.

Where is the chat data stored?

On your own server or on the EGroupware Rocket.Chat server in a German data centre. The conversation is transmitted encrypted.

Can I start a video conference from the chat?

Yes. With a right-click on a colleague in the status app you send a chat message or start a video conference directly.
