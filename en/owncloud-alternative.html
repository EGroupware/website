EGroupware and ownCloud – what suits your team?

Both are open source, both can be self-hosted. But they have a different focus: ownCloud mainly on files, EGroupware as a complete collaboration solution. Here we show what fits when – and what can be combined.

Start free trial What fits when?
Two focuses

Two open source projects, two strengths

We do not see other open source projects as competitors. What matters is what your team needs day to day.

ownCloud

Specialised in storing, syncing and sharing files – with sync programs for computers and smart­phones.

EGroupware

A complete collaboration solution: email, calendar, contacts, CRM, projects, tasks, time tracking and e-⁠invoicing – with an integrated file manager.

Decision guide

What fits when?

Most teams recognise themselves in one of the two columns.

A focus on files suits you if …

  • you mainly need to sync and share files.
  • email, calendar and contacts are already well covered.
  • many files need to be available offline on computers.

EGroupware suits you if …

  • you need appointments, email, contacts and projects in one place.
  • files should be linked with customers, projects and mails.
  • you also want to cover customer contacts, time tracking or e-⁠invoicing.
Combining

Together rather than against each other

Both projects rely on open standards such as WebDAV and can be extended with Collabora Online for collaborative document editing. We also offer Collabora Online for Nextcloud and ownCloud installations.

We are happy to discuss in person whether and how your existing installation can be connected with EGroupware.

Frequently asked questions about EGroupware and ownCloud

Does EGroupware have file storage too?

Yes. The file manager stores files centrally, shares folders with users and groups and creates sharing links – in the Enterprise Line and the cloud with password, expiry date and versions.

How do I access the files in EGroupware from my computer?

In the browser or as a network drive via the open standard WebDAV. There is no separate sync program as with ownCloud.

Can I use Collabora Online with ownCloud or Nextcloud?

Yes. We also offer Collabora Online from our cloud for other groupware installations, such as Nextcloud or ownCloud. Contact us.

Can EGroupware be self-hosted like ownCloud?

Yes. EGroupware runs on your own server – as the Community Edition without licence fees or as the Enterprise Line with manufacturer support. Alternatively, you use the EGroupware Cloud from Frankfurt am Main.
