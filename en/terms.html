The terms consist of three parts: the general product agreements and scope of services (Part A), the general terms and conditions for the EGroupware Enterprise Line (Part B) and the product agreement on data exchange (Part C).

Part A: General product agreements and scope of services

1. EGroupware cloud packages

1.1 User accounts. The number of user accounts is unlimited.

1.2 Concurrent users (CCU) and storage. Maximum number of users who can be logged in to EGroupware at the same time, and included storage, according to the cloud packages offered. The storage is available for the EGroupware database, files and emails.

1.3 Email accounts, spam filtering and virus protection. The EGroupware email application for any IMAP accounts is included. EGroupware email hosting (EGroupware GmbH as email provider) is optionally available; the storage depends on the respective cloud package. Email accounts are managed via user management in EGroupware. Spam filtering and virus protection via SpamTitan (mail­gateway.egroupware.org) are included in all EGroupware email accounts.

1.4 Licences for Collabora Online Office. Collabora Online Office is integrated into EGroupware. A licence is required for every user who is to have access to the Collabora application. Collabora only permits named licences or licences based on the number of documents edited simultaneously. The licences must be booked in addition to the cloud package.

1.5 Use of Rocket.Chat. Rocket.Chat is integrated into EGroupware. Rocket.Chat requires an additional database and storage, so its use must be booked in addition to the cloud package. A fair use principle applies; the price depends on the number of users in Rocket.Chat.

1.6 Video conferences with Jitsi / BigBlueButton. EGroupware GmbH provides a Jitsi server (jitsi.egroupware.org) for use in the EGroupware Cloud. The Jitsi server is rented from KiwiTalk.de but does not store any personal data. Like Jitsi, BigBlueButton is integrated into EGroupware and can be used for video conferences from the calendar or the status application. EGroupware does not provide its own BigBlueButton server but recommends booking via BBBserver.de. EGroupware GmbH asks to be sent the partner ID so that it can be registered as a reseller. This incurs no additional costs for the customer; it only refinances development costs.

1.7 Data backup. Daily data backup in the data centre in the form of snapshots going back 30 days, plus a maximum of 12 weekly snapshots. All backups are deleted automatically when the retention periods expire.

1.8 Access. Secure HTTPS connection to the EGroupware data instances.

1.9 Data centre operation. Certified data centre facilities, high-speed internet, high availability through multi-level system redundancy.

1.10 Contract term. Minimum contract term of 1 month with automatic renewal by 1 month unless objected to or otherwise agreed or offered.

1.11 Payment. Payable in advance for the contract term unless otherwise agreed or offered.

1.12 Cancellation. Written cancellation for annual and multi-year contracts with automatic renewal 3 months before the end of the contract, unless otherwise agreed.

2.1 User accounts. Maximum number of active or concurrent users (CCU) according to the respective package.

2.2 Number of installations. 1+1: one production instance plus one additional instance for testing and training purposes.

2.3 Updates. Version, bug fix and security updates are included within the agreed contract period. If configured accordingly, updates are installed automatically.

2.4 LDAP | AD | UCS | Samba – support with migration and integration. Support with the installation, migration or integration of further services such as LDAP or Active Directory is provided individually by agreement on the basis of support budgets.

2.5 EGroupware mail server packages and spam filtering. Support with migration to EGroupware mail server packages (Dovecot including push) is provided individually by agreement and on the basis of support budgets. Spam filtering via SpamTitan from EGroupware GmbH (mail­gateway.egroupware.org) can optionally be booked, billed on the basis of the number of email accounts and aliases.

2.6 Collabora Online Office, Rocket.Chat, Guacamole. Support with the installation of further services such as Collabora Online Office, Rocket.Chat or Guacamole is optional, by agreement and on the basis of support budgets.

2.11 Contract term. Minimum contract term of 12 months with automatic renewal by 1 month unless objected to or otherwise agreed or offered.

2.12 Payment. Payable in advance for the contract term unless otherwise agreed or offered.

2.13 Cancellation. Written cancellation for annual and multi-year contracts with automatic renewal 3 months before the end of the contract, unless otherwise agreed.

2. Safety notes and recommendations

2.1 Synchronisation with mobile devices can incur considerable costs depending on your mobile device’s tariff. This applies in particular to travel abroad with regard to roaming costs incurred there. EGroupware GmbH is not liable for any resulting damage. Check your tariff conditions carefully and ask your mobile provider for further information.

2.2 Synchronising data from mobile devices or desktop clients to EGroupware or vice versa always involves the risk of over­writing or deleting data. Make sure that you create an appropriate backup, in particular before an initial data synchronisation.

2.3 EGroupware includes a variety of strategies to prevent accidental deletion of data. In particular, enable history logging in the Calendar, Address Book and InfoLog applications and versioning in the File Manager. Not all applications include these precautions; in particular, using the import/export module requires appropriate knowledge and training to ensure that data is not accidentally deleted or changed.

2.4 Data synchronisation results vary considerably depending on the client and depend on the technical properties of the client, the content scope of the communication protocol and the scope of the EGroupware implementation. EGroupware’s team collaboration features can therefore only be transferred to the client to a limited extent. Limitations on clients include, for example, invitations of appointment participants, conflict handling for overlapping appointments and others.

2.5 Please note that on some smart­phones, an operating system update results in complete data deletion. Create a backup before updating your operating system and read the manufacturer’s documentation.

3. Desktop and smart­phone clients for data exchange, third-party clients

3.1 Please read the documentation and supplementary information of the respective manufacturers carefully and follow their recommendations. Back up your mobile device before starting an initial data synchronisation.

3.2 Compared with EGroupware’s collaborative features in the browser, considerable functional limitations are in some cases to be expected on mobile devices.
