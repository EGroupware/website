One idea since 1996

EGroupware started as a solution for a training agency that had to coordinate staff development programmes across Germany. It has grown into a complete online office – built consistently on open standards and digital sovereignty.

Our history

Grown from a concrete problem

In 1996 a training agency was missing a tool to coordinate appointments, participants and programmes across many locations. The solution that emerged was made open – and grew with every customer who contributed their own requirements.

Today EGroupware is a complete online office with more than twenty modules, running day-to-day business in companies, administrations and educational institutions. The ambition has stayed the same: software that makes work easier without tying people to a single vendor.

Location

Developed and hosted in Germany

Development, hosting and support all happen in Germany. The cloud runs in two data centres in Frankfurt am Main – for data protection and redundancy. Telephone support is available in German and English, and training can be delivered on site.
