Privacy Policy

Information on the processing of personal data pursuant to Art. 13 GDPR. Last updated: November 2026.

1. Summary

By itself, this website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking tools, or advertising services. It does not load fonts, scripts or videos from third-party servers. Cookies are only set if you open the live chat (see section 4). We only process personal data that you provide to us yourself – for example via a form, the live chat or by email – and in the technically required server logs.

3. Hosting and server logs

This website is operated by IONOS SE, Elgendorfer Straße 57, 56410 Montabaur, Germany (in containers or in the IONOS Managed Kubernetes service). We have concluded a data processing agreement with IONOS pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR.

Each time a page is accessed, your browser automatically transmits data that is stored in server logs:

  • IP address
  • date and time of the request
  • page or file requested
  • referrer URL (the previously visited page)
  • browser type and version, operating system

We need this data to deliver the website, keep it secure and detect errors or attacks. The legal basis is our legitimate interest pursuant to Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. The logs are not combined with other data or used to analyse user behaviour. They are deleted as soon as they are no longer required for these purposes.

Data is transmitted in encrypted form via HTTPS (TLS).

4. Storage in your browser and live chat

Light and dark mode

If you switch between light and dark mode using the icon in the header, we store this choice in your browser’s local storage (entries egw-theme and egw-theme-sys). The entries only contain the selected mode, are not transmitted to us and remain stored until you delete them in your browser. As you explicitly choose this function yourself, no consent is required (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG). No cookies are set for this.

Live chat

For the live chat we use the open-source software Rocket.Chat on our own server pole.egroupware.org. The chat is only loaded when you click the chat button in the bottom right corner – no connection to it is made before that. From this click on, the chat sets the cookies rc_is_widget and rc_room_type and stores a random visitor ID in your browser’s local storage so that a conversation you have started is kept when you change pages. As you explicitly open the chat yourself, no consent is required (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG).

If you use the chat, we process your name, your email address and your messages in order to answer your enquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR where your enquiry relates to a contract, otherwise our legitimate interest in answering enquiries (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). We delete chat histories after 90 days unless they are still needed for an ongoing enquiry or due to statutory retention obligations.

5. Contact by email, phone or fax

When you contact us, we process the information you provide (e.g. name, contact details and your request) in order to handle your request. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR if your request relates to a contract or its initiation, otherwise our legitimate interest in answering enquiries (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). For the storage period, see section 6.

6. Forms on this website

Forms are transmitted to our server in encrypted form. Your details are stored as a contact in the address book of our own EGroupware system, where our team handles them; they are not passed on to third parties. Mandatory fields are marked; without them we cannot handle your request. A hidden field only serves to block spam and contains no personal data.

Contact form

Via the contact form, we process your name, email address, company, phone number, the selected topic, the approximate number of users and your message. We use this information to answer your request, advise you and, if you wish, make you an offer. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (pre-⁠contractual measures), otherwise our legitimate interest in answering enquiries (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR).

Requesting a trial

To set up your trial instance, we process your name, company, email address, the desired address of the instance and your optional description. We use this data to set up the trial instance, support you during the trial period and – if you wish – convert it into a contract. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (pre-⁠contractual measures).

Cancelling a contract

To process your cancellation, we process your name, customer number, email address, phone number, country, reason for cancellation and your optional message. We need this information to assign the cancellation, confirm it and wind up the contract. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) and (c) GDPR. This data is stored in accordance with commercial and tax retention periods (up to ten years).

Storage period for enquiries and trial instances

We generally store data relating to enquiries for one year. If no contract is concluded during this time, we then delete it. We generally keep trial instances for 30 days after the trial period ends, so that you can continue seamlessly if you place an order, and then delete them. If a contract is concluded, we store the data for the term of the contract and beyond that only where statutory retention obligations apply. If you would like us to delete your data sooner, a short message to info@egroupware.org is enough – we will do so without delay.

News­letter in the forms

In the contact and trial forms, you can also sign up for the news­letter. We only add you if you explicitly tick the corresponding box. Section 7 applies to this.

7. News­letter

We only send our news­letter with your consent (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR). Only your email address is mandatory; your name is optional. After signing up, you receive an email with a confirmation link; only then is your subscription complete (double opt-⁠in). We log the sign-⁠up and confirmation in order to be able to prove your consent.

We use Klick-Tipp to send the news­letter. The provider is Klick-Tipp Limited, 15 Cambridge Court, 210 Shepherd’s Bush Road, London W6 7NJ, United Kingdom. Your data is stored on Klick-Tipp’s servers. We have concluded a data processing agreement with Klick-Tipp. An adequacy decision of the European Commission (Art. 45 GDPR) applies to the United Kingdom and is valid until 27 December 2031.

Klick-Tipp records whether a news­letter was opened and which links in it were clicked, and allows recipients to be grouped by interest or customer relationship (tagging). This helps us tailor the news­letter to its recipients.

You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in every issue or by email to us, thereby with­drawing your consent. We then delete your data from the mailing list. To make sure we do not contact you again by mistake, your email address may remain on a block list (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). More information: Klick-Tipp privacy policy.

8. Links to other providers

This website links to services of other providers, for example videos on YouTube, our social media profiles, GitHub or Digistore24 order pages. These are simple links: only when you click a link does your browser connect to the respective provider, who is then responsible for any further processing. Their privacy policies apply.

Some products are offered via Digistore24 GmbH, St.-Godehard-Straße 32, 31139 Hildesheim, Germany, as a reseller. When you order there, Digistore24 is your contractual partner and responsible for processing your data: Digistore24 privacy policy.

  • erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • withdrawal of consent with effect for the future (Art. 7 (3) GDPR)

Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR): Where we process your data on the basis of our legitimate interest (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR), you may object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation. We will then no longer process the data unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds or the processing serves legal claims. You may object to processing for direct marketing purposes at any time without giving reasons.

Simply contact info@egroupware.org or the address above. You can reach our data protection officer directly at daten­schutz@egroupware.org.

Right to lodge a complaint: You may lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR).
