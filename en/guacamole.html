Guacamole

With Apache Guacamole you reach your office PC, terminal server or other servers from home directly in the browser – embedded in EGroupware. EGroupware manages login and connections, secured with two-⁠factor authentication if you wish.

SPECIALGuacamole highlight

Remote access in the browser – with single sign-⁠on and a second factor

Guacamole provides RDP and VNC desktops via HTML5 directly in EGroupware. Login runs through EGroupware via OpenID Connect, and administrators manage the connections centrally. You can also secure access with a second factor – such as a passkey, Windows Hello or YubiKey, included in the Community Edition since EGroupware 26.8.

Features

Secure access to the office PC

A fully-fledged workplace at home – with nothing but the browser.

A fully-fledged workplace

Guacamole is more than screen sharing or remote maintenance: you work entirely on the remote computer. Demanding specialist applications keep running on the office PC, even if the computer at home is less powerful – and need no licence of their own there.

No open ports

Access via RDP is fast without having to open the RDP ports to the outside. Instead, the connection runs through EGroupware and your browser.

Back­ground on RDP security (heise, German)

RDP, VNC and SSH

Besides RDP, Guacamole supports VNC and SSH. This lets you continue existing sessions on the remote desktop (VNC) or maintain server consoles such as the EGroupware server remotely (SSH).

Access based on rights

Administrators decide which users may access which desktops and which security level is required for it, for example two-⁠factor authentication.

Reduce data protection risks

Setting up a VPN connection on private computers is time-consuming and involves GDPR risks, because files can end up on unsecured laptops. With Guacamole the data stays on the office PC.

Set-⁠up

How Guacamole gets into your EGroupware

With your own installation, Guacamole runs as a component on the same server as EGroupware. The administrator sets up OpenID Connect and the connections to the computers; users then see the remote PC directly in EGroupware. We can also offer a solution in hosting – this requires some configuration and coordination with your IT.

Frequently asked questions about Guacamole

Which connection types does Guacamole support?

RDP for Windows desktops and terminal servers, VNC for existing desktop sessions and SSH for server consoles.

Do I have to open RDP ports in the firewall?

No. The remote computers are accessed via EGroupware and the browser, so the RDP ports stay closed to the outside.

Is Guacamole also available in the EGroupware Cloud?

With your own installation, Guacamole runs on the EGroupware server. In hosting a solution is possible that requires some configuration and coordination with your IT – please get in touch.

Can the second factor be switched off in the company network?

Yes. In the Enterprise Line, an IP range such as your company network can count as the second factor, so the additional prompt is not needed there.
