Contacts

Keep your contacts up to date. Manage your personal and company address books centrally and in line with the GDPR, using EGroupware. Synchronise your contacts to your smart­phone and laptop and reach them wherever you are.

All modules at a glance / Contacts

Organise contacts and save time

EGroupware lets you set up separate address books for groups or departments. Contacts can be categorised using distribution lists and custom fields.

Your own contact fields

You can easily create your own data fields and adapt EGroupware to your needs without any programming knowledge.

Putting the GDPR into practice – professional data protection

The policy application in EGroupware lets you define rules for each individual address book. Which fields have to be filled in? Who inside the company may access them? Who is allowed to see a contact's private phone numbers? A practical detail: the rules can be printed and filed directly in the record of processing activities.

Share contacts with other address books

Contacts can be shown in another address book through a share, with read or write access. Specific fields can be excluded by policy, or marked as mandatory.

Address book permissions

Agile rights management lets you decide centrally who may access which data. Your addresses are also available for mail merges and for synchronisation with smart­phones and mail clients.

Personalised mail merges and emails

Templates with placeholders let you create business documents, mail merges and emails in a structured way – without moving your data into other software.

Synchronise your contacts

Keep your contacts with you at all times, on your laptop as well as on your smart­phone. Beyond that you have many options for exchanging contacts in various formats.
