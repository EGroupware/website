Operation

Platform and infrastructure

What EGroupware runs on – in the customer's own data centre or in our cloud.

SERVER PLATFORM

Univention Corporate Server

Univention GmbH, Bremen

UCS manages users, permissions and services centrally. EGroupware is available in the Univention App Center and takes users and groups directly from UCS.

EGroupware on UCSApp Center

INFRASTRUCTUREGREEN HOSTING

IONOS

IONOS SE, Montabaur

The EGroupware Cloud runs on IONOS Managed Kubernetes. IONOS is one of the major European providers of hosting and cloud infrastructure.

EGroupware Cloudionos.com

Documents

Office in the browser and on the desktop

Edit documents together in the browser or with the office suite at your desk – both from Collabora, both based on LibreOffice.

ONLINE OFFICEOPEN SOURCE

Collabora Online

Collabora Productivity Ltd, Cambridge

Edit texts, spread­sheets and presentations together in the browser, straight from the EGroupware file manager.

Online officecollabora­office.com

DESKTOP OFFICEOPEN SOURCE

Collabora Office

Collabora Productivity Ltd, Cambridge

The office suite for business use on the desktop, with long-term, cross-platform support. Collabora is one of the leading contributors to LibreOffice.

Collabora Office

Communication

Meetings and chat

Chat with your team and start video conferences from the calendar and chat – self-hosted or as a service from Germany.

CHATOPEN SOURCE

Rocket.Chat

Rocket.Chat Technologies Corp., Wilmington (USA)

Team chat with channels, direct messages and video calls, integrated into EGroupware with sign-⁠in via OpenID Connect.

Chat in EGroupwarerocket.chat

VIDEO SERVICE

bbbserver

invokable GmbH, Remscheid

GDPR-compliant meeting platform based on BigBlueButton for lessons, webinars and conferences, with servers in Germany.

Video conferencingbbbserver.de

VIDEOOPEN SOURCE

Jitsi

8x8, Inc., Campbell (USA)

Open-source video conferencing that is secure, easy to use and easy to host yourself.

Video conferencingjitsi.org

VIDEO SERVICE

KiwiTalk

salmacis.com GmbH, Pullach

KiwiTalk runs the Jitsi video conferences of the EGroupware Cloud and also hosts dedicated Jitsi instances for customers – with a high level of data protection.

Video conferencingkiwitalk.de

Connectivity

Telephony and remote access

Start calls from the address book and access remote computers – without additional software.

TELEPHONYGOLD PARTNER

Placetel

BroadSoft Germany GmbH (Cisco), Cologne

Cloud phone system with more than 150 features. EGroupware has been a Gold Partner since 2021 and connects the phone system directly to contacts and calendar.

Phone integrationplacetel.de

REMOTE ACCESSOPEN SOURCE

Apache Guacamole

Apache Software Foundation

Remote desktop gateway for VNC, RDP and SSH. Access runs entirely in the browser; no plugins or client software required.

Guacamole in EGroupwareguacamole.apache.org

Synchronisation

Appointments and contacts on every device

Using the open standards CalDAV and CardDAV, Outlook and Android devices stay in sync with EGroupware.

OUTLOOKOPEN SOURCE

Outlook CalDav Synchronizer

Generalize-⁠IT Solutions OG, Vienna

Outlook plugin that synchronises appointments, tasks and contacts between Outlook and EGroupware.

Setupcaldav­synchronizer.org

ANDROIDOPEN SOURCE

DAVx⁵

bitfire web engineering GmbH, Bad Vöslau

Brings CalDAV, CardDAV and WebDAV to Android: contacts, calendars and tasks in sync, plus direct access to your EGroupware files.

Synchronisationdavx5.com

Migration & archiving

Bring your data along and keep it safe

Move existing mailboxes to EGroupware and archive emails in an audit-proof way.

MIGRATION

audriga

audriga GmbH, Karlsruhe

Migration service for emails, files and groupware data – from a single account to millions of accounts, from other groupware or mail servers to EGroupware.

audriga.com

ARCHIVINGOPEN SOURCE

Benno MailArchiv

LWsystems GmbH & Co. KG, Bad Iburg

Archives emails in the back­ground in an audit-proof way in line with the German GoBD. Archived messages can be found quickly but can no longer be changed.

benno-mailarchiv.de
