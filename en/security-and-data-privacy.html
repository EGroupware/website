Security and data privacy

In the EGroupware Cloud, we take care of servers, updates and backups in Frankfurt am Main. On your own server, you keep full control. In both cases, EGroupware protects access and data with the same features.

How EGroupware protects access and data

These features apply in the cloud and on your own server. Features only available in the Enterprise Line are marked.

Two-⁠factor login

Besides a code from an app, also passkeys, Windows Hello and hardware keys such as YubiKey or Nitrokey (WebAuthn). Included in the Community Edition since EGroupware 26.8.

Permissions by group

You set read, edit and delete rights per application and group. Users can come from Active Directory, LDAP or Univention, and login also works via single sign-⁠on with OpenID Connect.

Encrypted content

Encrypt and sign emails with PGP or S/MIME. In the Enterprise Line, you can also encrypt confidential tasks, such as access credentials, with PGP.

Traceable for audits

All administrative changes are logged automatically. In the Enterprise Line, you also record on whose behalf and for what reason a change was made.

ENTERPRISE LINE

Data protection by rule

Address book policies define who may see which data, when contacts are deleted and when they need to be verified – ready to print for your record of processing activities.

Control over files

Shares for external people, in the Enterprise Line with password and expiry date. Plus versioning with restore and, on S3 storage, encryption at rest with 30-day deletion protection.

ENTERPRISE LINE

Application firewall

With freely definable rules, you decide who may log in, when and from where. For example: from outside the internal network only with a second factor, or the other way round, the internal network itself counts as the second factor. Or members of a group can only log in between 8 am and 6 pm.

Application passwords

For smart­phones, sync apps or the REST API, you use separate tokens instead of your password. Each token can be limited to individual applications, given an expiry date and revoked at any time.

Operation

Cloud or your own server

Who takes care of what depends on the operating model. You can switch at any time.

EGroupware Cloud

  • Two data centres of our partner IONOS in Frankfurt am Main, on our own storage systems
  • Access exclusively encrypted via HTTPS
  • We handle installation, updates, monitoring and high availability
  • 7 daily and 12 weekly backups in both data centres, optionally also in an S3 storage of your choice
  • Files on S3 encrypted at rest

More about the EGroupware Cloud

Your own server

  • All data stays in your own IT infrastructure
  • Firewall, encryption and access controls according to your requirements
  • No access by cloud providers or foreign jurisdictions
  • Operation, updates and backups are up to you – with our support if you wish

Frequently asked questions about security and data privacy

Where is my data stored in the EGroupware Cloud?

In two data centres of our partner IONOS in Frankfurt am Main, on our own storage systems. The operator is EGroupware GmbH, a German company.

How can I protect my team's accounts further?

With two-⁠factor login: via a code from an app, a passkey, Windows Hello or a hardware key. As an administrator, you can make the second factor mandatory for all users.

Can I also keep my own backup outside the cloud?

Yes. Every backup can additionally go to a third S3 storage, with a hosting provider of your choice or in your own data centre. More under data backup and disaster recovery.

Is self-hosting more secure than the cloud?

Not automatically. On your own server you have full control, but you are also responsible for updates, backups and monitoring. In the cloud, we take care of that for you.
