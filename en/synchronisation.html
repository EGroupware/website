Features

Synchronisation

Sync calendars, contacts, tasks, emails and files with your smart­phone, tablet and the programs on your computer – via open standards such as CalDAV, CardDAV, WebDAV and IMAP, and additionally via the ActiveSync protocol. That way your data is with you even without an internet connection.

  • Offline tooData is stored on the device

SPECIALOpen standards first

No special app, no lock-⁠in

EGroupware syncs calendars and contacts via CalDAV and CardDAV. iPhone, iPad and Mac speak these open standards out of the box, as do Thunderbird and eM Client. For Android, there is the open source app DAVx⁵. You set up nothing that ties you to EGroupware – and you can connect your devices to other services in the same way.

Case study: Jena city council with Managed DAVx⁵

Devices and programs

How to connect your devices

Usually the address of your EGroupware, your user name and a password are all you need. You choose in the settings which calendars and address books are synced.

iPhone, iPad and Mac

The built-⁠in Calendar, Reminders and Contacts apps speak CalDAV and CardDAV directly – on the Mac just as on iPhone and iPad, plus Apple Mail via IMAP. You add your EGroupware account once in the settings, no extra app needed.

Android with DAVx⁵

Android does not support CalDAV and CardDAV out of the box. We recommend DAVx⁵ by bitfire, available in the Google Play Store (paid) or free via F-⁠Droid. For organisations there is Managed DAVx⁵ with centrally distributed settings.

Thunderbird and eM Client

Both programs speak CalDAV, CardDAV and IMAP natively – our recommendation for the desktop on Windows, macOS and Linux.

Outlook

With the CalDAV Synchronizer, you sync calendar and address book between EGroupware and classic Outlook for Windows in both directions.

More options

Email, files and the ActiveSync protocol

Email via IMAP

Use your mailboxes with any email program via IMAP, sending via SMTP. Folders and read messages stay the same every­where.

Files as a drive

Via WebDAV, you mount the EGroupware file manager as a drive on Windows, macOS or Linux and work with it like a local folder.

The ActiveSync protocol

For devices and programs without CalDAV, EGroupware provides the ActiveSync protocol via the open source software Z-⁠Push (eSync). A single account setup syncs email, address book, calendar and, if you wish, tasks. In the Enterprise Line, you can also manage profiles for the devices.

On the go

Or simply in the browser

Since EGroupware 26.2, the interface adapts to every screen. On smart­phone and tablet, you work in the browser with all applications and data in real time – including helpdesk, projects and time tracking, which a sync does not transfer.

More on mobile access

TipSECURITY

Set up a separate application password for each device instead of storing your login password. If a smart­phone gets lost, you only revoke that one token.

Frequently asked questions about synchronisation

Do I need a separate EGroupware app?

No. You sync calendars and contacts with the programs already on your device, on Android with DAVx⁵. Every­thing else you use in the browser.

Does the new Outlook work?

The CalDAV Synchronizer is an add-⁠in for classic Outlook for Windows. The new Outlook does not support such add-⁠ins. As an alternative, we recommend Thunderbird or eM Client.

Which data is synced?

Calendars and tasks via CalDAV, contacts via CardDAV, emails via IMAP and files via WebDAV. You choose in the EGroupware settings which calendars and address books go to a device.
