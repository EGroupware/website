Features

Kanban

Manage sales, projects, support or software development on a Kanban board that makes your work visible and limits the tasks running at the same time. Contacts, tasks and tickets from EGroupware go straight onto the board as cards.

Columns that react to changes

When a developer sets a ticket to the status "Testing", the card moves into the "Testing" column automatically – without anyone touching the board. The other way round, a column changes the entry as soon as you drag a card into it: for example, it sets the status or enters you as the person responsible. This keeps the board and the data in step without duplicate work.

Features

Make workflows visible

From incoming sales leads to the completed ticket – on one board.

Design the board freely

Every new board starts with the columns "New (Pool)", "In progress" and "Done". You can change the number, names and colours of columns and swimlanes at any time, sort them by drag-⁠and-⁠drop and collapse them in one click.

NEW Up to 99 instead of 9 columns and swimlanes per board.

Data from all applications

Contacts, tasks, tickets and projects go onto the board as cards. Drag an entry onto the board or create it right there.

New entries automatically

Define which new entries land in the first column automatically – in sales, for example, all contacts in the address book "Sales incoming", in development the tickets of a category or product version.

Responsibilities per step

Give a column fixed responsible people or take over those of the swimlane. If you wish, completed entries can disappear from the board automatically.

Permissions for board and swimlane

You decide which groups have access to a board. For each swimlane you also define who may move entries within it.

Boards as favourites

Save your boards as favourites and switch between sales, support and development in one click.

NEW Favourites can be arranged hierarchically – handy if you manage a lot of boards.

In practice

Kanban in sales, projects and support

The core of Kanban is to make tasks visible and limit the work in progress: as soon as capacity becomes free, an employee picks the next task from the pool. One example is acquiring new customers in sales – you adapt the process, address books, task types and statuses to your own workflows. Keep the board simple and improve it with your team's feedback.

Frequently asked questions about Kanban

Is Kanban included in the Community Edition?

No. Kanban is part of the EGroupware Enterprise Line. Our comparison of the Community Edition and the Enterprise Line shows the differences between the editions.

Which entries can I put on the board?

Contacts, tasks, tickets and projects from EGroupware. You decide which applications a board allows when you set it up.

Do I have to move cards by hand after every change?

No. You define how entries move to another column automatically when they change, for example in their status. The other way round, moving a card can change the entry.

Who may move cards?

You decide which groups have access to a board. For each swimlane you can also define who may move entries within it.
