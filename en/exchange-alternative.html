Switching

How to move from Exchange

Four steps – and we are happy to accompany each of them.

1. Try it

Start the EGroupware Cloud free for 30 days and try out the most important workflows with a small team.

2. Move your data

We help migrate emails, calendars and contacts – including moving your email domain.

3. Connect devices

Outlook connects via IMAP and the CalDAV Synchronizer – or you use Thunderbird or eM Client, which speak the open standards natively. Smart­phones and tablets connect via CalDAV and CardDAV or the ActiveSync protocol.

4. Switch off

Once every­thing runs, switch Exchange off. Thanks to open standards, your data always remains readable – even without EGroupware.

Frequently asked questions about switching from Exchange

Can I keep using Outlook?

Yes. You retrieve emails via IMAP, and the CalDAV Synchronizer syncs appointments and contacts between Outlook and EGroupware in both directions.

What do you recommend instead of Outlook?

Programs that speak the open standards natively: Mozilla Thunderbird (open source) or eM Client. Both connect directly to EGroupware via IMAP, CalDAV and CardDAV – without an add-⁠on like the CalDAV Synchronizer that Outlook needs. Or you work straight in the EGroupware email client in the browser, which is directly connected to calendar, contacts and files.

Do smart­phones and tablets work?

Yes, via the open standards CalDAV and CardDAV or via the ActiveSync protocol, which EGroupware provides with the open source software Z-⁠Push. This lets you connect iOS and Android devices – on Android, for CalDAV and CardDAV, for example with the DAVx⁵ app.

How does my data get from Exchange to EGroupware?

We help migrate emails, calendars and contacts and move your email domain. Contact us and we will plan the switch together.

Is EGroupware available for your own server, like Exchange Server?

Yes. EGroupware runs as a cloud service from Frankfurt am Main or on your own server – as the Community Edition without licence fees or as the Enterprise Line with manufacturer support. If you want to manage Active Directory or LDAP centrally, run EGroupware as an app on Univention Corporate Server (UCS) – UCS includes user and domain management.
