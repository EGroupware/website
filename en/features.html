Frequently asked questions about features

Can I switch individual modules off?

Yes. EGroupware is consistently modular. You activate only what your team needs and can extend at any time – for example start with the calendar and add CRM and projects later.

Does EGroupware sync with Outlook or an iPhone?

Yes. Calendars and contacts synchronise through the open CalDAV and CardDAV standards. Email runs over IMAP and therefore works with virtually any mail client.

Do external guests need an account for video meetings?

No. Guests join via a link in the browser. An EGroupware account is not required.

Can I create my own fields?

Yes. Custom fields, categories and types are created through the interface – no programming knowledge needed.
