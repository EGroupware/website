Features

Vidoteach – video-based learning

With Vidoteach (formerly smallPART), pupils and students learn directly in the video: they ask questions, give feedback and mark areas of the picture exactly at the right moment. Teachers answer comments, set exam questions and evaluate them largely automatically.

Questions that jump to the right video

A new question type branches the learning path: depending on the answer chosen, Vidoteach jumps to one of several target videos. This lets you build learning paths on which everyone continues with the video that matches their answer.

Features

Learning directly in the video

Create a course, insert a video, describe the task – that is all it takes.

Comments in the running video

Learners see the video and the task and comment with one click in the running video, colour-coded green, red or white. Teachers see all comments, answer them and release them.

Interactive lecture

Learners ask questions about specific moments in the video as comments marked in red. Teachers answer them, set them to green and decide whether everyone can see them – so all benefit from the others' questions.

Analyse processes

Whether lab work, operating equipment or a driving school situation: teachers add comments and observation tasks to the video. Learners respond or mark important elements in the picture, such as sources of danger or controls.

Exams in the video

Since EGroupware 21.1, Vidoteach combines videos and exams: learners see the video together with the exam questions and a countdown. You can choose single-choice, multiple-choice and open questions with an answer note for marking.

Largely automatic evaluation

Vidoteach sets the start time and display duration of a question automatically; compulsory questions and random order are possible. You mark open questions manually with a score, and all questions, results and comments can be exported.

Scientifically supported

The tool emerged from a cooperation between working groups at the University of Tübingen, TU Kaiserslautern and EGroupware GmbH. After each learning cycle it is developed further based on feedback on usability and learning effectiveness.

Learning platforms

Vidoteach in Moodle, OpenOLAT and Ilias

Vidoteach needs an EGroupware installation as the basis for login and database. Participants can, however, use Vidoteach exclusively in the learning platform of your choice, provided it embeds courses via LTI (Learning Tools Interoperability) – such as Moodle, OpenOLAT or Ilias. As part of EGroupware, Vidoteach supports SAML authentication and single sign-⁠on, so learners access it via their school's or university's system.

Frequently asked questions about Vidoteach

Can Vidoteach be embedded in Moodle?

Yes. Via LTI you publish Vidoteach courses in learning platforms such as Moodle, OpenOLAT or Ilias. Participants then work entirely in their familiar platform.

Which question types are available?

Single-choice, multiple-choice and open questions with an answer note for marking. New is a question type that jumps to a different video depending on the answer.

Does Vidoteach need an EGroupware installation?

Yes, EGroupware is the basis for login and database. You can also run Vidoteach on your own server with EGroupware.
