How billing works

You pay for the people who are logged in right now

Create an account for every employee, freelancer and part-timer. The price follows how many of them are logged in at the same time, not how many accounts exist.

An example

A team of 20: ten work full time and are logged in most of the day, ten drop in occasionally. You book around 12 to 14 simultaneous logins, not 20.

  • Unlimited user accounts on every plan
  • Simultaneous logins can be topped up at any time
  • Need extra for a training day? Just ask

Frequently asked questions about pricing

What does CCU mean?

CCU stands for concurrent users. You do not pay per created account but by the number of people working in the system at the same time. For teams with shift work or occasional users that is considerably cheaper than a licence per head.

Can I try EGroupware first?

Yes. The cloud version can be tested free of charge and without obligation for 30 days. The Community Edition is permanently available for download without licence fees.

How does the support contingent work?

Support is purchased in advance as a contingent in 15-minute units. Contingents are valid for two years from purchase; unused time expires afterwards.

Can I move from the cloud to my own server later?

Yes. Because EGroupware is built on open source and open standards, changing the operating model works in both directions. Our support team assists with migrations on request.

Are there special rates for schools and associations?

Yes. eCloud NGO and eFlat School are dedicated tariffs for educational institutions, associations and non-profit organisations.

Not sure which model fits?

Tell us how many people work at the same time and which modules you need – we will calculate both options for you.
