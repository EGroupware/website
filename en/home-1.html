What is EGroupware?

One platform instead of many isolated tools

EGroupware brings every­thing a team needs day to day into a single web interface. All information is connected: the appointment knows the customer, the task knows the project, the email is filed with the right record. That saves time, avoids duplicate data entry and makes knowledge available across the team.

  • Runs in any modern browser on any operating system – nothing to install on the desktop.
  • Modular by design: activate only the applications you actually need.
  • Open interfaces to Active Directory, LDAP, Univention, CalDAV and CardDAV.
  • Scales from a five-person office to an administration with thousands of accounts.

Fresh design, better accessibility, AI assistance

The current generation brings a completely reworked, responsive interface and makes everyday work noticeably faster.

  • Fresh, fully responsive design for desktop and mobile devices.

  • AI assistance with hybrid search across full text and RAG.
  • AI tools for emails and text fields, right inside your workflow.
  • Rolling releases with new version numbering – features arrive continuously, not once a year.

E-⁠invoicing

Compliant invoicing – without extra software

Since 2025 EGroupware has included its own application for the electronic invoice that is legally required in Germany. It supports the XRechnung and ZUGFeRD formats in line with EU standard EN 16931 – generated from the data already held in your CRM and time­sheets.

  • XRechnung and ZUGFeRD to EN 16931
  • Linked directly to contacts, projects and time­sheets
  • Audit-proof filing in the file manager
