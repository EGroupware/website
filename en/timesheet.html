Features

Timesheet

Record working time for tasks, tickets and projects – from simple time recording to hour quotas within a project. Filters and reports turn it into a record of work done, for example as an attachment to an invoice.

Working time by start and stop

Start time recording when you begin work and stop it at the end – or enter your day as individual times. If you like, EGroupware automatically suggests the next time slot of the day as the start time. That way, your daily record builds up almost by itself.

Features

Record and evaluate times

Transparency for the team and a record of work done for your clients.

Times for project and task

Book hours worked to a project, a task or a ticket. EGroupware compares target and actual times – useful for the overview and for post-calculation.

Custom fields and status

Record additional information in custom fields or with a status, for example billed or paid. Changes to times are logged and are only visible to those responsible.

Billable times in view

Mark entries as invoiced or as not to be invoiced. A filter shows all times that still belong on an invoice.

NEW Since EGroupware 26.6: "billable" filter and status for e-⁠invoicing – there, selected time­sheets become invoice items directly (Enterprise Line).

Filters as favourites

Filter the hours by customer, project, employee, task or time period and export the result. Save frequent combinations as a favourite.

NEW Favourites can be arranged hierarchically.

Permissions and team leads

You decide who may create, see and edit time­sheets. Employees might see only their own times, while the team lead sees those of the whole team. Once billed, a status blocks further changes.

Records from templates

Create time records as a PDF with your logo – from template documents with placeholders that you design in Collabora Online. Sample templates are in the file manager, and all placeholders are listed in the side menu.

Interfaces

Times from on the road and from other systems

With the mobile view, field staff book their times directly from a smart­phone or tablet. For exchanging data with other systems, there is import and export in CSV format, scheduled if required, for example from an ERP system or a service management tool. Time­sheets can also be created via the REST API.

Frequently asked questions about the timesheet

Can I also record times from my smart­phone?

Yes. With the mobile view of EGroupware, you book times in the browser of your smart­phone or tablet, for example right at the customer's site.

Who can see my time­sheets?

You decide that with permissions. A typical set-⁠up: employees see their own times, the team lead sees all times of their team.

Can I create an invoice from the times?

Yes, with e-⁠invoicing in the Enterprise Line: selected time­sheets become invoice items and are marked as invoiced. Alternatively, you create a time record as a PDF attachment from a template.

Can times be imported from other programs?

Yes, via CSV import, scheduled if required. A REST API is also available.
