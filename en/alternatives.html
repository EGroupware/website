Time to switch from Microsoft?

Frequently asked questions about switching

Is EGroupware an alternative to Microsoft 365?

For many teams, yes. EGroupware covers typical office tasks: appointments, email, files, collaborative document editing via Collabora Online, chat and video conferencing. The differences: open source code, hosting in Germany and billing by concurrent users instead of per user. If you depend on specific features of Word, Excel or Teams, compare the feature set before­hand.

Is EGroupware an alternative to Google Workspace?

Yes, if you need calendar, email, contacts, files and shared documents in one place and want your data stored in Germany. EGroupware runs in the cloud from Frankfurt or on your own server.

Can I keep using Outlook and my smart­phone?

Yes. You retrieve emails via IMAP, and the CalDAV Synchronizer syncs appointments and contacts with Outlook. Smart­phones and tablets connect via CalDAV and CardDAV or the ActiveSync protocol.

What do you recommend instead of Outlook?

Programs that speak the open standards natively: Mozilla Thunderbird (open source) or eM Client. Both connect directly to EGroupware via IMAP, CalDAV and CardDAV – without an add-⁠on like the CalDAV Synchronizer that Outlook needs. Or you work straight in the EGroupware email client in the browser, which is directly connected to calendar, contacts and files.

Can EGroupware be combined with other open source software?

Yes. EGroupware relies on open standards such as IMAP, CalDAV, CardDAV and WebDAV and offers a REST API. Collabora Online for document editing is already integrated.
