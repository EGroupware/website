Frequently asked questions about the partner programme

What does it cost to take part?

Nothing. Taking part in the partner programme is free and non-⁠binding.

How is my commission settled?

Automatically via Digistore24: when someone buys via your partner link, the commission is credited to your Digistore24 account.

Do I already earn a commission for registering?

No. Registering with Digistore24 – even for several products or under different email addresses – does not earn a commission. A commission is only paid when someone buys a product via your partner link.

Can I look after my customers myself?

Yes. Many of our resellers advise their customers and set up EGroupware for them – with a direct line to the EGroupware team. Contact us if you would like to work more closely with us.
