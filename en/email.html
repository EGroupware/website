Features

Email

Personal mailboxes and shared team mailboxes in one interface – in the browser, on your smart­phone and in your mail program. Because the email client is linked to the address book, tasks and file manager, every piece of information ends up in the right place straight away.

Coloured flags and your own tags

The familiar red "Important" flag is joined by further colours. You create your own flags as categories for emails and manage them yourself. This lets you sort your inbox by your own topics and see at a glance what needs doing next.

Features

Handle email as a team

From your own mailbox to the department's shared info mailbox.

Multiple accounts and team mailboxes

Add several email accounts and use a department's shared mailboxes, such as an office or info mailbox. That way everyone stays up to date.

Sharing and permissions

Share individual folders with colleagues and decide who may access an account – without passing on login details. Administrators can also switch on the out-⁠of-⁠office message for a colleague who is off sick.

Linked to every application

Attach files straight from the file manager without uploading them first. Save an email as a task in one click – linked to the sender and with all attachments.

Large files via link

Send large files as a sharing link or share a folder into which external partners can upload files in return.

NEW If attachments exceed the configured size, EGroupware automatically sends a download link instead.

Encrypt and sign

EGroupware supports S/MIME and PGP. With S/MIME the keys are stored centrally: the public key in the address book, the private key in the email profile. PGP keys stay in the browser.

NEW PGP signatures of incoming emails are verified directly in the browser.

Create S/MIME certificates directly

Create the certificate signing request (CSR) in EGroupware, submit it to your certificate authority and import the issued certificate together with the intermediate certificate. For testing, generate self-signed certificates.

NEW Since EGroupware 26.8 – also for renewing expiring certificates.

Signatures, filters, out of office

Alongside a central company signature with placeholders for the user's details, you create your own signatures. You manage filter rules, forwarding and out-⁠of-⁠office messages yourself.

Find faster, send safely

Combine search terms with AND, OR, + and −. If you like, unread emails always stay at the top of the list.

NEW With many recipients in To and Cc, EGroupware warns you and offers to move them to Bcc. Distribution lists can be resolved into individual recipients before sending.

Mark as spam

With the SpamTitan integration, hosting customers mark emails as spam or wanted directly and maintain black and white lists. The filter learns as it goes. We offer spam and virus filtering as a service, for self-hosted installations too.

Synchronisation

Your email in every program

EGroupware relies on open standards: your mailbox sits on an IMAP server, and Thunderbird, eM Client and smart­phone mail apps access it alongside the browser. Since version 26.9 the email client also speaks the modern JMAP protocol, for example with mail servers such as Stalwart. For devices that do not speak open standards, the ActiveSync protocol is available via the open-source software Z-⁠Push.

In the EGroupware Cloud, with Univention Corporate Server (UCS) or with our mail server packages (Dovecot), every new user automatically gets an email account. You manage aliases, forwarding and storage space directly in EGroupware.

Frequently asked questions about email

Can I also read my email in Thunderbird or on my smart­phone?

Yes. Your mailbox is reachable via IMAP, and Thunderbird, eM Client and smart­phone mail apps access it alongside the browser. For devices without IMAP, the ActiveSync protocol is available via Z-⁠Push.

Can a team use a shared mailbox?

Yes. You add shared mailboxes such as an info or office mailbox for the whole department. Permissions determine who may access them, without passing on login details.

Does EGroupware support encrypted email?

Yes, with S/MIME and PGP. Since version 26.8 you can request and import S/MIME certificates directly in EGroupware.

How do I send files that are too large for an attachment?

As a sharing link from the file manager. If an attachment exceeds the configured size, EGroupware automatically turns it into a download link.
