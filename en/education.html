Everyday use

What schools and universities organise with EGroupware

From cover planning to lab equipment: the typical tasks in education and research.

Calendar and communication

Email, address book, personal calendars and calendars for classes or work groups. You can easily subscribe to external calendars, such as the school holidays of your federal state.

Rooms and equipment loans

Book rooms, vehicles and lab equipment right in the calendar. If you wish, learners send requests themselves and the people in charge approve the booking.

Files for work groups

Distribute course material and collect homework. External people get access via share links, and you edit documents together with Collabora Online.

Learning with video

With VIDOTEACH, learners comment right in the video, work on assignments and take tests – also embedded in Moodle, OpenOLAT or Ilias via LTI.

Tasks and time recording

Plan homework and projects as tasks, and easily record the working hours of student assistants.

Fits your IT

Users and groups come from LDAP, Active Directory or Univention, and your existing permission management stays. EGroupware is used in the browser and on mobile devices.

Timetable

Bring Untis and WebUntis into the calendar

With UntisSync, you import time­tables and appointments from Untis or WebUntis into the EGroupware calendars. Teachers see lessons, cover and their own appointments in one place. Time­tables are imported in full every night, and UntisSync checks for new cover every 20 minutes.

UntisSync is developed and maintained by a developer who also works as a teacher and uses the application every day at their own school in the EGroupware Cloud. The source code is openly available on GitHub.

Untis and WebUntis are product names of Untis GmbH.

Frequently asked questions

Are there special conditions for schools and universities?

Yes. eFlat School is a flat rate for educational institutions with an unlimited number of users and concurrent logins. For smaller institutions, eCloud NGO is available at a reduced rate. For universities and self-hosted installations, we prepare an individual offer.

Can learners book rooms and equipment themselves?

If you allow it, yes. Depending on your workflow, learners book directly or send a request that a responsible person confirms.

Does VIDOTEACH work in our learning platform?

VIDOTEACH can be embedded in Moodle, OpenOLAT and Ilias via LTI. An EGroupware installation serves as the basis for login and data.

Can we import our timetable from Untis?

Yes, with UntisSync you import data from Untis or WebUntis into the EGroupware calendars. We install it on request at no extra charge and help you set it up from your support package.
