Webinars on EGroupware 21.1

Dear EGroupware user,
The development of EGroupware 21.1 has continued over the last few weeks and some bugs have been fixed. Many thanks to all testers of the new version.
If you are interested in testing EGroupware 21.1 in the cloud, just let us know.
First EGroupware 21.1 packages for self-installation will be released Thursday (18.3.2021).
If you want to test EGroupware 21.1 in the self-installation, please change the tag from "20.1" to "21.1" in /etc/egroupware-docker/docker-compose.override.yml after the packages have been released. We inform in the EGroupware forum under the category "Announcements".
The first version does not yet contain all new functions, the feature for the EGroupware firewall policy will only be published in the next release candidate.

New webinars on EGroupware 21.1

In our new webinars you have the opportunity to get to know the new functions and to ask our experts questions directly. In addition, we would like to point out various exciting events with workshops where you can take away practical knowledge on the use of EGroupware 21.1.

  • Kanban Board in EGroupware Date: Thursday, 25 March 2021 from 15.00 (planned 60 minutes) Registration: EGroupware 21.1 - New Kanban application The new Kanban application with practical examples: New Customer Acquisition, CRM & KeyAccount Management, Projects & Task Management, as well as Software Development & Ticket Management. This Webinar is only in German language.
  • smallPART: Online exams and tests with videos Date: Tuesday, 30 March 2021 from 15.00 (planned 60 minutes) Registration: EGroupware 21.1 - smallPART exams and tests with videos Get to know the application smallPART! Webinar with focus on video-based exams Practical examples from teaching with Arash Tolou & Christoph Thyssen (TU Kaiserslautern). This Webinar is only in German language.
  • Telephony integration (CTI) and cooperation EGroupware with Placetel Date: Thursday, 15 April 2021 from 15.00 (planned 60 minutes) Registration: EGroupware - CTI - Integration using the example of Placetel Presentation of the CTI integration with Placetel, call lists, calendar, CRM view A Placetel representative will also be available to answer any questions you may have about Placetel itself. This Webinar is only in German language.
  • Features EGroupware 21.1 - Webinar in English, if you are interested.The date is not fixed yet.
